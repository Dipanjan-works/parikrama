ARTISAN QUARTER

Kumartuli: Sculpting Clay & Soul

"Where straw and Ganges silt transform into the Mother of the Universe."

In Kumartuli's rustic workshops, master sculptors invoke life into Maa Durga's third eye during Chokhu Daan on Mahalaya dawn — a moment of sacred reverence that initiates Kolkata's festive season.

Read Time: 4 mins North Kolkata
EVENING RITUALS

Dhunuchi Naach & Rhythmic Frenzy

"Fragrant frankincense smoke and hypnotic dhak cadences."

As Ashtami evening Aarti echoes across illuminated pandal arches, dancers balance smoking terracotta dhunuchi vessels filled with burning camphor, swaying to thunderous drum rhythms.

Read Time: 3 mins Ashtami Aarti

HERITAGE FOOD

Pujor Swad: The Midnight Feast

"From crisp Phuchkas to fragrant Ashtami Khichudi Bhog."

Midnight pandal hopping is powered by street gastronomy — crisp tangy Phuchkas, hot Kathi rolls, spicy Fish Kabiraji, and kulhad chai under bright hanging festival lights.

Read Time: 5 mins Gastronomy
FAREWELL RITUAL

Bijoya Dashami & Sindoor Khela

"A bittersweet celebration of vermilion powder and emotional farewell."

On Dashami afternoon, women in white and red-bordered sarees smear scarlet vermilion powder on each other's cheeks before Maa Durga's immersion procession to the Ganges.

Read Time: 4 mins Bijoya Dashami
